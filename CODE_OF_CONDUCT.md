# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want DentOS to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of age, background, identity, experience level, language, or program of study.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, in French or English
- Welcome newcomers and answer questions patiently
- Give and accept feedback graciously
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, insults, or personal attacks
- Sharing others' private information, including any patient or student personal data
- Posting copyrighted exam or course material without the right to share it
- Other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate.

## Reporting

Report concerns by contacting the maintainer through the email on the maintainer's GitHub profile ([@liamelkaim](https://github.com/liamelkaim)). Reports are handled confidentially.

This policy is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
